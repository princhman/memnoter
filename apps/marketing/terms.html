The software and these terms

The Memnoter apps are free and licensed under the MIT License. That license is what lets you use, copy and modify the software. These terms cover the copies we distribute, this website, and the optional sync service at sync.memnoter.com.

The sync server is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0. You can self-host it. If you run a modified server as a service, that license requires you to publish your changes.

Beta

Memnoter is in beta. It can crash, lose a note, or sync badly. The desktop and mobile apps keep a daily backup of notes and images for the last 7 days, and you can make your own copy. Do not rely on Memnoter as the only copy of work you cannot afford to lose.

Your notes

Notes, flashcards, images, PDFs and review history stay on your device unless you turn sync on. Sync encrypts that content on the device before upload. We cannot read it, and we cannot recover a sync passphrase you forget. The privacy policy says what the server does store.

Accounts

You do not need an account to study on one device. Sync uses your email to send a one-time sign-in code. You choose the passphrase. You are responsible for keeping it, and for the email account you sign in with.

On iPhone and iPad, Settings, then Sync, then Delete account removes the sync account and the encrypted cloud copy. On Mac, Windows and Linux, Delete cloud data removes the encrypted cloud copy and keeps the account. Local notes stay on the device either way.

Using the service

Do not attack the site or the sync service, probe them for a weakness, or overload them. Do not upload content you have no right to store. Do not use Memnoter to harm someone else. We may suspend a sync account that is used this way. Your local notes are still yours.
