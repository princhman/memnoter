Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Data on your device

You can use Memnoter without an account. Your notes, flashcards, images, review history, and imported PDFs and annotations are stored on your device. Memnoter uploads this content only if you turn on sync.

Optional sync

We use your email address to identify your sync account and send a one-time sign-in code. We keep an account ID linked to your email address and use a sign-in token to keep you signed in. We use your IP address to limit sign-in attempts.

Your device encrypts synced content before uploading it. The server stores encrypted changes, snapshots and files, along with details such as document IDs, the order of changes, and file sizes and upload times. It also stores information your device uses to check your passphrase. The passphrase and encryption key stay on your devices. We cannot read your synced content.

Deleting your data

Choose Sign out under Settings → Sync to stop syncing on one device. Signing out keeps your encrypted data on the server.

On iPhone and iPad, open Settings → Sync → Delete account to remove your sync account and encrypted data from the server. Your local data stays on your devices. We keep a deletion record tied to an account ID so old sign-ins cannot reconnect.

On Mac, Windows and Linux, Settings → Sync → Delete cloud data… removes the encrypted cloud copy and keeps your account. For help deleting the account, email us.

Updates and service providers

Cloudflare hosts this site and the sync service, stores synced data and sends sign-in emails. Cloudflare receives your IP address when you connect to the site or sync service. The sync service records technical logs to help diagnose problems.

The app checks GitHub for new releases up to once a day while in use. The desktop updater also checks GitHub at startup or when you check manually, and downloads available updates there. GitHub receives your IP address when your device connects.

Memnoter has no ads, tracking or product analytics. It uses no third-party SDKs for advertising, tracking or analytics.
