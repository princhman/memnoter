Common questions

Where are my notes stored?

On your device. Memnoter works without an account. If you turn on sync, your notes are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded.

How do I get my notes on another device?

Open Settings, then Sync, on each device. Sign in with the same email address and enter the same sync passphrase. Notes, images and review history then stay up to date across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Linux.

I forgot my sync passphrase

The passphrase never leaves your devices, so we cannot recover it. You can reset sync and choose a new one. The notes on your device stay, but the old encrypted cloud copy cannot be recovered.

Can I restore an earlier version of my notes?

Yes. Memnoter makes a backup every day and keeps the last seven. Open Settings, then Backups, and choose the day to restore. You can also make a backup at any time from the same place.

How do I make a flashcard?

Write Question :: answer on a line of its own. Use ::: for a card that is asked in both directions, or wrap a word in {{ }} to hide it. A dot appears beside each card in your notes.

How do I stop sync or remove my data from the server?

Sign out under Settings, then Sync, to stop syncing on that device. To have your account and its encrypted data removed from the server, email us from the address you signed in with.
